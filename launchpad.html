What we are building next.

Every step is the same vault, wearing a different hat. No dates, because we ship when it works. In order of what we hit first.

  1. 01 · next
    Quantum-safe wallet with in-browser signing. Generate the 24 words, sign transfers straight from the web, watch the key rotate on chain. A Phantom that a quantum computer cannot empty.
  2. 02
    DAO signer. Replace a multisig with a rotating hash key. Every proposal signs once and the key that voted it dies. A stolen seed cannot vote for the DAO ever again.
  3. 03
    Treasury vault for projects. Put a project treasury behind the vault instead of a hot wallet. Movements need a fresh signature; nothing on a laptop can drain it.
  4. 04
    Upgrade authority as a service. Any Solana program can hand its upgrade key to a vault. Your contract survives a quantum break of the whole chain.
  5. 05
    Time-locked vaults. Vest a founder allocation or a partner grant behind a schedule that only the vault can unlock. No hot key that anyone can compromise midway through.
  6. 06
    NFT custody for collectors. Send a Metaplex asset to a vault. It stays there until a one-time signature moves it. Wallet drainers stop working.
  7. 07
    Signed messages under Winternitz. Sign a note with a key that cannot be forged. A message from a project years from now still verifies as theirs.
  8. 08
    Our own curve, immutable. Replace the Meteora curve with one whose upgrade key is burned. The last elliptic-curve key in the whole stack disappears. Audited before it ships, because there are no second chances with immutable code.
  9. 09
    Winternitz SDK. A library any Solana project can drop in to put their own mint, treasury or upgrade key behind the vault. Everyone on Solana gets to skip the quantum apocalypse.
